# RehabIQ

RehabIQ is an Agentic AI-powered, bilingual tele-rehabilitation coach designed to support patients and clinicians with remote rehabilitation monitoring and guidance.

## Overview

RehabIQ uses computer vision and sequential pose analysis to assess movement quality during home-based rehabilitation exercises. By interpreting body posture and movement over time, the system can help identify incorrect motion patterns, monitor progress, and provide actionable feedback to improve recovery outcomes.

The platform is intended to make rehabilitation more accessible, personalized, and scalable—particularly for patients who need support outside of traditional in-clinic settings.

## Core Concept

RehabIQ combines:

- Vision-based movement analysis
- Sequential pose tracking over time
- AI-driven coaching and decision support
- Bilingual interaction for broader accessibility
- Tele-rehabilitation workflows for remote patient engagement

This enables the system to act like an intelligent digital coach that can evaluate exercise execution, recognize performance issues, and guide users through rehabilitation routines with contextual feedback.

## Key Features

- Bilingual user experience
- Tele-rehabilitation support
- Vision-based pose analysis
- Sequential movement evaluation
- Agentic AI guidance for rehabilitation coaching
- Personalized monitoring of exercise quality and progression

## Use Case

RehabIQ is designed for rehabilitation scenarios where patients perform guided exercises at home while receiving remote feedback. It can support clinicians by reducing manual monitoring burden and help patients stay engaged and consistent with their therapy plan.

## Goal

The goal of RehabIQ is to bring intelligent, affordable, and accessible rehabilitation coaching to patients through AI-enabled remote monitoring and feedback.
